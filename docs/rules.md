**I. Suggested Activity Rules**

Owners should:

* Check in to the league at least once every three weeks during the off-season.  
* Check in at least once every two days during the regular season.  
* Check in at least once a day during the rookie draft.  
* Vote in all league polls.

## **If you're not interested in following these "suggestions", why are you playing dynasty?** 

---

**II. League Configuration**

This league will consist of one 12 team conference with no divisions. Since this is a dynasty league, all players will be kept until the owner involves that player in a transaction (either a trade or a drop/add).

## **Scoring**

* Passing Touchdowns: 4 points each  
* Passing Yards: .04 points each  
* Pass Interceptions Thrown: \-2 points each  
* Passing 2 Pointers: 2 points each  
* Rushing Touchdowns: 6 points each  
* Rushing Yards: 0.1 points each yard  
* Rushing 2 Pointers: 2 points each

* Receiving Touchdowns: 6 points each  
* Receiving Yards: 0.1 points each yard  
* Receptions: .5 per reception for RB, 1 for WR, and 1.25 for TE  
* Receiving 2 Pointers: 2 points each

* Fumbles: \-2 points each  
* Fumble recovery for TD for offensive player: 6 points each

## **Rosters**

Rosters consist of 26 players. The commissioner will expand this to 30 in the offseason after the first run of free agency (in mid-March) to allow for rookies to be added. All rosters must be cut back down to 26 by June 1\. 

Players are locked at the kickoff of their game. An owner may change the starting lineup status for a player whose game has not yet started. Our league will not use any team defenses or kickers. The starting lineup cannot include any players on a bye week or skill position players listed as out or inactive according to the league’s final injury report prior to game day.

Each week, you must fill the following 10-man starting lineup:

* 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 3 Flex, 1 SF (QB/RB/WR/TE)

## **III. Drafts**

## **Startup Draft**

Startup draft picks may be traded. A 4-hour time clock will be used during the startup draft, with the timer being off between 12:00 AM EST and 8:00 AM EST. The startup draft will be a snake draft; teams are not required to draft a full roster in the startup draft.

If an owner’s pick is skipped, that owner should contact the commissioner with his pick as soon as possible. The commissioner will then process the transaction. Once a draft pick is made, that pick is final and official.

Draft picks cannot be traded until everybody has paid the initial fee.

## **Rookie Draft**

The 4 round rookie draft will be held the second Monday following the NFL draft each spring in every season.

The rookie draft will not be a snake draft. Only rookies (both drafted and undrafted) are eligible to be selected. The rookie draft will use a 4 hour clock for each pick. The draft clock will be turned off at 12:00 AM EST and will be started again at 8:00 AM EST.

If an owner’s pick is skipped, that owner should contact the commissioner with his pick as soon as possible. The commissioner will then process the transaction. Once a draft pick is made, that pick is final. 

The draft order will be determined by regular season finish, except for teams that made the playoffs. Those teams will be seeded in reverse order of the final standings (with 6th place receiving 1.07 and league champion getting 1.12).

Trading of draft picks is allowed as long as the year is paid for. e.g. 2026 buy-in covers three years \- 2026, 2027 and 2028 picks can be traded.

## **IV. Regular Season**

The regular season will last from week 1 of the NFL season until week 14 of the NFL season. 

## **V. Trades**

All trades are executed upon acceptance by both owners involved on the league website. The trade deadline will occur at the kickoff of the week 12 NFL games. Trading will re-open the Tuesday immediately following the conclusion of the Week 17 games.

At the conclusion of each trade, rosters may exceed 26 players but lineup submission will be locked until roster requirements are met again (cut back down to 26).

If at least two owners think a deal is unfair, they have 48 hours to bring that to the attention of the commissioner. The commissioner will then make a decision regarding the validity of the trade. Owners are encouraged to post on the league message board or group chat in order to explain and discuss any recent trades.

## **VI. Waivers**

Waivers will be decided using a blind bidding system. Each owner will be given $1,000 of FAAB to bid on players through the waiver wire system. Bids will be processed every Wednesday at 8 PM EST. After waivers are processed using the blind bidding system, waivers will then be first come, first serve (FCFS) until 1 PM EST Sunday.

Waivers will run through the playoffs for all teams, including those not advancing to the playoffs.

Waivers will be open to blind bidding (only) during the off-season. There will be no FCFS during the offseason. There will be one waiver run prior to the NFL draft (in late March or early April). Waivers and free agency will then close during the NFL draft and rookie draft. Owners will still have the ability to drop players in order to make their draft selection.

Waivers will be open to blind bidding (only) starting two weeks after the conclusion of the rookie draft until the beginning of the NFL preseason. There will be no FCFS during this portion of the offseason. Once the pre-season begins, waivers will follow the weekly blind bidding system as usual \- including FCFS. 

If a player is dropped accidentally, that owner has one hour to contact the commissioner in order to reverse the transaction.

## **VII. Playoffs**

The regular season will end after week 14 and the playoffs will begin in week 15 and go through week 17\.

Six teams will qualify for the playoffs. The final spot will be decided by total points scored. Two-way tiebreakers will be broken by the winner of the regular season matchup between the two teams that tied. 

Week 15 will see match-ups between the 3 and 6 seeds and the 4 and 5 seeds. Seeds 1 and 2 will receive first round byes. In Week 16, the \#1 seed will play the lowest remaining seed. The \#2 seed will play the other remaining team in the conference semifinals.

## **VIII. Toilet Bowl**

The eight teams that do not advance to the playoffs in each conference will participate in the Toilet Bowl. The teams in this bracket will be seeded one through eight based on total points scored, with the number one seed going to the team with the worst total. Tiebreakers will be used if necessary as above.

The winner of this bracket will receive a compensatory pick between rounds one and two (1.13) and will be a tradable commodity.

## **VIX. Annual Fees**

## The buy-in for this league will be $40. Initial buy-in is $120 and covers three years.

## **X. Awards**

* Overall pot: $480 ($40\*12)   
* First place: $300 (remainder of the prize pool)  
* Second place: $120  
* Third place: $60

## **XI. Rule Changes**

Rule changes will take place during the off-season. Suggestions can be made to the commissioner at any point. The commissioner does have the right to institute a change that may be minor but be for the better of the league immediately. Each rule change will require a majority of all players (7/12 players).

Rule discussions will take place immediately after the season ends. Proposed rule changes will be set as poll questions and will run until February 1st.

## **XII. Tanking**

Tanking will not be tolerated in this league. Tanking is defined as follows:

* You must submit your best lineup every week. Sitting good players in bad matchups is permitted, but in cases where the commissioner questions a judgment call, a reasonable explanation must be provided.  
* You are not permitted to start players on bye weeks.  
* If a player is ruled out 24 hours in advance of a game, that player must be removed from the starting lineup.

*If an owner is found to be in violation of any of the three rules above, the punishments will be as follows:*

* 1st offense: Warning  
* 2nd offense: Loss of 1st round pick  
* 3rd offense: Expulsion from league

**XIII. Orphaned Teams**

At the conclusion of the fantasy season, any owner planning to leave the league should notify the commissioner as soon as possible.

The commissioner will fill any orphaned teams with new owners. If there are multiple orphaned teams in the league, the commissioner will host a dispersal draft. All new owners will participate and will choose from players and picks owned by the previously orphaned teams.
